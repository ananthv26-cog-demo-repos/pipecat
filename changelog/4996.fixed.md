- Fixed `LLMAssistantAggregator` losing words from an interrupted assistant turn or prepending them to the next one. The output transport releases each `TTSTextFrame` as its audio plays, but those frames could still be queued at the aggregator when the `InterruptionFrame` arrived: the interruption either flushed them from the queue or overtook them, closing the turn before they were aggregated. The aggregator now keeps every `TTSTextFrame` it receives and closes the interrupted turn only after the queued text has been aggregated, so the context matches what the user heard and the bot no longer repeats itself.
